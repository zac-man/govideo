Core Creative Pillars
1. Visual De-contextualization (视觉去日常化)
Remove the object from its natural habitat (never shoot in a kitchen, wet market, or cluttered bedroom). Place it in vast, uninhabited, anti-human environments:

Locations: Icelandic black sand beaches, Northwest Gobi/Yardang landforms, foggy sea cliffs, brutalist concrete ruins, or minimalist empty art galleries.
Color & Lighting: Desaturated Teal & Orange, cold overcast Nordic twilight, heavy natural 35mm film grain, high-contrast chiaroscuro, silhouette backlighting.
Camera Work: Extreme low-angle ground-tracking shots, macro shallow depth-of-field shots capturing manufacturing flaws (plastic mold lines, rust, rough stitches, oil stains) as if they were bespoke artisan textures, and 120fps slow-motion.
2. Existential High-Fashion Casting (高傲且疲惫的模特设定)
Styling: Oversized tailored wool coats, dark avant-garde trench coats, sharp suits, polished leather shoes.
Performance: Chiseled, melancholic face; looking 45 degrees into the distance; zero worldly destination ("if the destination is going home, the vision is too small; he must only journey toward the unknown").
Interaction: Treat the cheap item like a sacred relic, a battle armor, or a bespoke V12 supercar.
3. Pretentious Copywriting Translation (缺陷升华黑话库)
Never mention low price, convenience, or durability. Rebrand every physical flaw or mundane trait into pseudo-philosophical or aerospace/luxury jargon:

Coined Luxury Naming: Combine 2-3 pretentious Chinese characters (曜, 臻, 境, 穹, 驭, 释, 极, 赫) + Alphanumeric code (SL, GT, Pro, X-01) + Edition (敞篷版, 高定概念系, 旗舰座舱).
Flaw-to-Feature Formula:
No roof / exposed -> "360-degree panoramic sky-curtain & all-natural aerodynamic ventilation system" (全系标配360度全景天幕与全方位自然通风系统)
Open cargo bed / large pocket -> "Grand-scale mobile executive space" (大格局移动商务空间)
Hole in the middle (plastic stool) -> "Central aerodynamic pressure-relief vortex & minimalist negative-space geometry" (中置空气动力学排压气孔与极简留白几何美学)
Thin plastic / rough fabric -> "Lightweight polymer unibody / frontier protective armor against worldly chaos" (轻量化聚合物一体成型 / 抵御世俗喧嚣的前沿铠甲)
Three wheels / two straps -> "Three rounds of destiny's trials / Y-axis dual-vector structural bridge" (历经三轮考验 / Y字双向稳固桥接系统)
4. Dual-Track Audio Contrast (双轨声音反差)
Alternate between two distinct voiceover styles:

Track A (Director's Meta-Commentary / 导演拆台旁白): Casual, deadpan, self-aware explanation of the commercial clichés ("First, find a road 18,000 miles from home... slow motion plus deep nonsense equals luxury").
Track B (Luxury Trailer Voiceover / 低音炮大片配音): Deep, resonant, gravelly baritone speaking philosophical nonsense over Hans Zimmer-style orchestral strings, pulsing synth bass, or lone cello.

Gotchas
Never modify the cheap object's appearance to look golden or futuristic: The humor and art rely entirely on keeping the object 100% authentic, cheap, rusty, or plastic (e.g., keep the blue paint peeling on the tricycle, keep the mold seams on the red plastic stool) while making the environment, lighting, model, and camera work look like a $10M commercial. Emphasize this in AI prompts so the generator doesn't turn the object into actual gold/carbon fiber.
Balance Track A and Track B: Pure luxury narration without the director's self-aware deconstruction loses Wang Zuo's signature meta-humor. Always include both the "how-to cliché breakdown" (Track A) and the "deadpan luxury demonstration" (Track B).
